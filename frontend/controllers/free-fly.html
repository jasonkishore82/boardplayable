FLYPAD

Your phone is the controller. Hold it the way you'd like to fly (portrait or landscape). Tilt sideways to bank and turn, tip it forward or back to dive or climb, and hold Boost to go fast.

SETTINGS

Tilt is re-centred automatically each time you take off, using however you're holding the phone at that moment.
