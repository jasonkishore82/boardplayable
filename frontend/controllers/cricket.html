BATPAD

Your phone is the bat's handle. Hold it upright, top up and screen towards you, with the blade hanging below it. The bat copies every turn of the phone, so swing it like a real bat: lift it back, then bring it down through the line of the ball.

SETTINGS

The way you hold the phone when you press Start (or Recenter) becomes your stance. At 40° the bat turns exactly as far as the phone; a smaller swing range makes a small turn of the wrist swing the bat further.
