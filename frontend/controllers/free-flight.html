FLIGHTPAD

Type the 4-letter code shown on the game screen you want to fly.

Your phone is the flight stick. Hold it comfortably, tilt left/right to turn and tip forward/back to dive/climb. Hold Boost to go fast.

SETTINGS

Tilt re-centres when you take off, using however you're holding the phone at that moment. Tap Recenter any time.
