RACKETPAD

Your phone is the racket. Hold it like the handle, screen towards you. Tilt it left or right to aim across their court. As the shuttle comes to you, swing it back and then through: a soft swing drops it short, a hard one from up high smashes.

SETTINGS

The way you hold the phone when you press Start (or Recenter) is straight down the court and the start of your swing. A smaller swing range means a smaller swing hits the shuttle.
